See the configuration Relay is running with

Settings now shows the effective environment as a read-only list, with copy and download as a .env file. Credentials and keys are never included.

Actions, and a faster way through the ledger

  • A new Actions screen lists every executed action and lets you mark one verified or record a failure.
  • Events can be searched, filtered by severity, sorted, loaded in pages and exported as CSV or JSON.
  • The dead-letter queue has search, select-all and bulk replay or resolve, with a confirmation before anything is dropped.
  • A live health indicator in the top bar, and more commands in the command palette.

  • Small text meets contrast requirements, and focus is always visible.
  • Page transitions and list entrances respect the reduced-motion setting.
